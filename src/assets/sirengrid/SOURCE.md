`nasr-city.json` is derived from the SirenGrid team's processed Nasr City data
(MahmoudNagiubX/SirenGrid, `data/processed/nasr_city`): road geometry from OpenStreetMap
(© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and WorldPop 2025 population zones. Coordinates are
projected to a 1000-unit-wide SVG space. The incident, unit and hospital routes are
shortest paths on that road graph for an illustrative scenario, not a recorded incident.
